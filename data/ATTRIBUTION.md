# Content attribution

The files in this folder are derived from [Monkeytype](https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype)
(GPL-3.0 — see https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype/blob/master/LICENSE):

- `quotes-en.json` ← `frontend/static/quotes/english.json` (6,488 quotes)
- `quotes-fr.json` ← `frontend/static/quotes/french.json` (1,107 quotes)
- `words-en.json` ← `frontend/static/languages/english.json`
- `words-en-1k.json` ← `frontend/static/languages/english_1k.json`
- `words-fr.json` ← `frontend/static/languages/french.json`
- `words-fr-1k.json` ← `frontend/static/languages/french_1k.json`

Individual quotes remain the property of their respective authors/sources
(as listed in each entry's `source` field). The punctuation/numbers
test modifiers are re-implemented from Monkeytype's word-generation logic,
not copied verbatim.
